Kokoro-82M (English voice synthesis) -- provenance and license notes
Compiled 2026-10-24 for the manabi-no-niwa asset pipeline (scripts/voice/generate_en.py).

What is used (development time only; none of this is shipped with the app)
  - Model weights : hexgrad/Kokoro-82M v1.0, converted to ONNX by thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
      kokoro-v1.0.onnx  (325,505,369 bytes, sha256 beb0d1848dee9a49da392cc3df26958d46cfa35d321edf434f52949153f0df3a)
      voices-v1.0.bin   ( 28,214,398 bytes, sha256 bca610b8308e8d99f32e6fe4197e7ec01679264efed0cac9140fe9c29f1fbf7d)
      from https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.1
  - Python runtime: kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 (MIT, see MIT-kokoro-onnx-LICENSE.txt), onnxruntime 1.31.0 (MIT),
                    phonemizer 3.4.0 (GPL-3.0) and espeakng-loader 0.2.4 (bundles espeak-ng, GPL-3.0) for text -> phonemes.
  - Voice         : af_heart (American English, female) by default.

License of the model
  - Apache License 2.0 (full text: Apache-2.0-Kokoro-LICENSE.txt, copied from https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/blob/main/LICENSE).
  - Hugging Face model card (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), metadata "license: apache-2.0", and the text:
      "This is an Apache-licensed model, and Kokoro has been deployed in numerous projects and commercial APIs."
  - Base model recorded in the model card metadata: yl4579/StyleTTS2-LJSpeech.

Training data statement (model card, "Data:")
  "Kokoro was trained exclusively on permissive/non-copyrighted audio data and IPA phoneme labels."
  Examples listed there: public domain audio; audio licensed under Apache, MIT, etc.; synthetic audio generated by closed TTS
  models from large providers (no synthetic audio from open TTS models or custom voice clones).
  Datasets with attribution requirements named by the author: Koniwa "tnc" (Japanese, CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (French, CC BY 4.0).
  Neither is English; the English voices used here are not stated to derive from them. VOICES.md names no dataset or
  license for any individual English voice. (Residual uncertainty: see docs/assets-pipeline.md, "未確認事項".)

Output
  The generated mp3 files are audio produced by running the model. The Apache-2.0 license of the weights, and the GPL-3.0 of
  espeak-ng/phonemizer (programs used to produce phoneme strings), do not extend to the generated audio. The mp3 files
  can be redistributed with the app. Giving credit (below) is not required for the output by the Apache-2.0 text, but is
  recommended and costs nothing.
